Your friends on Instagram, without the rabbit hole.

Unreeled is a free, open-source iPhone app for Instagram that shows the people you follow and nothing else: their posts newest first, their stories, your messages and your activity. No Reels feed, no Explore, no suggested posts, no ads.

How to install Source code

Unofficial

Unreeled isn't made or endorsed by Instagram or Meta. It talks to Instagram the way instagram.com does, from your phone. Instagram doesn't approve apps like this one, and it could restrict or disable an account that uses it. Use it at your own risk.

Install

In the EU, Japan and Brazil

From AltStore PAL, an alternative app marketplace for iPhone. Coming soon.

Anywhere else

Build it yourself with Xcode. With a free Apple ID, the app has to be reinstalled every 7 days.

  1. Install Xcode 27 or later and clone the repository.
  2. Copy Config/Local.example.xcconfig to Config/Local.xcconfig, and set your team ID and a bundle ID of your own in it.
  3. Open Unreeled.xcodeproj, pick the Unreeled for iPhone scheme and your iPhone, and press ⌘R.
  4. The first time: turn on Developer Mode on the iPhone (Settings › Privacy & Security, then restart), and trust your Apple ID under Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.

Private

Unreeled collects nothing. You log in on Instagram's own page, and your account goes only between your phone and Instagram. There are no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code. The only other request the app makes downloads the project's list of Instagram query IDs.

Privacy policy

How it works

Instagram's website loads nearly everything through GraphQL queries that it sends by ID. Unreeled sends the same queries, with the same parameters and headers, from a hidden web view on www.instagram.com. That way each request carries the website's cookies and the browser's own headers, and the session never leaves your phone. The screens on top are native SwiftUI.

When Instagram changes a query, its ID changes and the screen that uses it stops loading. The fix is one edit to the project's list of queries. Installed apps pick up the new list within 6 hours, or right away when a query fails, with no new version needed.

Questions

Could Instagram restrict my account?

It could. Instagram doesn't approve apps like this one, and its checks for automated behaviour look at more than how requests are made. Unreeled sends only requests the website itself sends, and nothing automated: no background likes, follows or scraping. Keep likes and follows at a human pace.

Why isn't it on the App Store?

App Review requires permission from the services an app uses. In the EU, Japan and Brazil, apps can also be distributed through alternative marketplaces after Apple's lighter check, notarization. That's how Unreeled is coming to AltStore PAL.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Unreeled is free software: you can share and change it under the terms of the GNU General Public License, version 3. Its code, its documentation and this website are on GitHub, where issues and pull requests are welcome.
